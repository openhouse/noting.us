2. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/sources`, then the running-minutes source detail. Point to stable segment IDs, source dates, visibility, consent, and public-use metadata.
3. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/bundles/current-shared-language`. Use the bundle sections: Rendered Markdown, Raw `page.md`, Metadata, Sources / Citations, Evals, Permissions, Wiki Export, Revisions, and Review History.
4. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/bundles/legal-bill-text-review`. Show `legalAdvice: false`, `needs_human_review`, source status date, warnings, revisions, and the human correction event.
5. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/bundles/story-bank-consent-rules` and show consent states plus public-use limits.
6. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/lineages` and separate city/state/SBJSA/CRS lineage lanes.
7. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/review` to show that bot drafts are not authority and human review is part of the product surface.
8. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/evals` to show source grounding, consent boundary, public/internal boundary, legal boundary, stale-status, and export-integrity checks.
9. Open `/u/fairrentnyc/export` and explain that `pnpm export:bundle legal-bill-text-review` writes a ZIP preserving sidecars and `export-manifest.json`.
