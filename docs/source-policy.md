# Source Policy

Demo fixtures must be safe synthetic, safe excerpt, redacted fixture, or public-source-derived material with attribution. Do not commit raw private archives, contact lists, sign-in sheets, private transcripts, lease terms, phone numbers, emails, vulnerable story details, or private photos.

Generated pages must preserve source IDs, source dates, bot attribution, review status, consent status, visibility, legal caution, and export sidecars. Import never equals publish.
